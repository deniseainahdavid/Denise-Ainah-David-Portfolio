Campaign Case Study: From Click to Conversion

Growth Calculator Campaign

The Goal

Encourage prospects to input their business numbers into a growth calculator to demonstrate how offshoring could support scalability, ROI, and long-term growth, shifting the conversation from cost to strategic growth.

What I Built

A conversion-focused landing page designed to guide users toward completing the growth calculator
Supporting social media content to drive traffic and introduce the growth narrative
A short-form explainer video, embedded on the landing page, to increase engagement and clarify the value of the calculator

All assets were designed to work together as part of a single campaign funnel.

Tools Used

  • HubSpot – landing page creation and lead capture
  • Canva – design of campaign visuals and social content
  • DaVinci Resolve – video editing for landing page explainer
  • ChatGPT & AI tools – campaign messaging and copy refinement
  • Meta Business Suite – content scheduling and campaign support

Campaign Flow

Social Media Content → Growth Calculator Landing Page → Calculator Interaction → Lead Capture for Follow-Up Conversations

This structure ensured traffic was guided toward a clear action rather than passive content consumption.

Outcome

  • Generated consistent website traffic during Q4 2025
  • Captured 10 qualified prospect leads through the growth calculator
